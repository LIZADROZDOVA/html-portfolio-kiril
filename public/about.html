About Me

I'm Kirill Drozdov — a professional chef, photographer and designer based in London. With over 22 years of experience in professional kitchens, I bring a strong understanding of food, hospitality and visual presentation into my creative work.

I create food and lifestyle photography, digital designs, websites, menus and brand visuals for hospitality and creative projects. I combine culinary experience with photography, design and technology to create clean, practical and visually engaging experiences.

My approach is simple: understand the idea, create the visual language and turn it into something clear, functional and memorable
